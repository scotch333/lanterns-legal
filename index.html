✦ Lanterns — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026, updated September 12, 2026 — applies to Lanterns on iOS and Android

Lanterns is a daily logic-puzzle game. Each day you solve one puzzle: place a lantern in every row, column, and colored region without letting any two touch. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information, on both the iOS and Android versions.

The short version

Lanterns is built to need essentially none of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data collection. The app has no servers of its own — your puzzles are generated on your device, and your streak, solve history, and settings live entirely on your device. Its one always-on network request is a once-a-day read of a small, public announcements file (see below), which carries nothing about you. Nothing about your play is sent to the developer or anyone else — with one optional exception on Android: if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games for leaderboards, your streaks and daily solve times are sent to Google under your Play Games profile (see "Leaderboards" below). Lanterns is fully playable without ever signing in.

Information Lanterns uses

On-device data. Your streak, solve history, daily completion, and app preferences are stored locally on your device, using each platform's standard local storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android). This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to the developer or anyone else.

Notifications (optional, off until you allow them; iOS and Android). If you turn on the daily reminder — or, on Android, the streak warning — Lanterns schedules a local notification on your device to nudge you about the day's puzzle. These reminders are generated on your phone, are not sent through any server, and involve no personal data leaving your device. You can turn them off at any time.

Sharing your result. When you tap Share, Lanterns hands a spoiler-free emoji grid and your solve time to your device's native share sheet so that you can post it wherever you choose. This happens only when you ask for it, and the result you share contains no personal information — and nothing is sent to the developer.

What Lanterns does not do

  • It does not talk to any server about you. Its only network request is described under "Announcements" below: a read of a public file, with nothing sent.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in. (Android leaderboards are optional and need a Google Play Games sign-in only if you choose them.)
  • It does not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • It does not contain advertising or ad-tracking.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not include third-party SDKs that collect your data.
  • It does not request your location, contacts, photos, or health data.
  • It does not sell or share your personal information.

Third-party services

Lanterns integrates no third-party services. The only exception is during beta testing:

  • Apple TestFlight (iOS beta only) — while Lanterns is distributed as a TestFlight beta, Apple may collect crash logs and basic usage information from the beta build to help diagnose problems, governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your personal information through this.
  • Google Play (Android beta/closed testing only) — while Lanterns is distributed through Google Play's testing tracks, Google may collect crash logs and basic usage information from the test build to help diagnose problems, governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your personal information through this.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, deleting the Lanterns app removes your streak, solve history, and preferences from your device. There is no server-side copy, because there is no server.

Permissions and your choices

You're always in control. On iOS, the only permission Lanterns may ask for is Notifications, for the optional daily reminder — you can grant or revoke it at any time in the iOS Settings app, and turning it off disables the reminder without affecting anything else. On Android, Lanterns asks for Notifications only when you switch on the daily reminder or the streak warning — never at launch — and it declares one further permission so those reminders can be re-scheduled after your phone restarts, and the standard network permission for the announcements read described below. Nothing else.

Announcements (iOS and Android)

Once a day at most, when you open the app, Lanterns reads a small public file that lists in-app announcements — a new game, a seasonal challenge, a note about a fix. It is a plain download of a static file hosted on GitHub Pages (scotch333.github.io/announcements). The request contains nothing about you: no account, no identifier, no information about your play, no cookies. As with visiting any web page, the host can see your device's IP address and a generic description of the app and operating system version, and nothing else. Lanterns keeps its last copy of the file on your device so it works offline, remembers only which announcements it has already shown you, and sends nothing back.

Leaderboards (Android, optional)

Lanterns for Android can show leaderboards through Google Play Games Services, but only if you tap Leaderboards in Settings and sign in to your Google Play Games profile. Until you do, no scores and no player identifier are sent — the app only checks, as any Play Games-enabled app does, whether Play services are available. Once signed in, Lanterns submits your best streaks (Lanterns, Mini Sudoku, Fireflies, Fuse and Daily Hard) and your daily solve times to Google, associated with your Play Games player ID, so they can appear on the leaderboards. That data is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and shown to other players as leaderboard entries under your Play Games name. The developer does not receive it. Signing out of Play Games stops any further submissions; removing the app from your Play Games profile, in the Play Games app, removes its data there. Everything else in Lanterns keeps working exactly the same whether or not you sign in.

Children's privacy

Lanterns does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app collects no personal data on the developer's behalf.
